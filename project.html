A Beginner's guide to Baking Pastries

This is the perfect starting point for anyone eager to master the art of pastry-making! Designed especially for beginners, it offers step-by-step instructions for creating everything from buttery croissants and puff pastry to fruit tarts, eclairs, and other delicious treats. Each recipe is supported by clear visuals that show what every stage should look like, helping you build confidence as you bake. To make learning even easier, links throughout the book link to short video tutorials that demonstrate key techniques in real time. With its simple instructions, helpful visuals, and interactive learning tools, this recipe book makes baking pastries approachable, rewarding, and fun for every home baker.

Here are the possible pastries you can make!
